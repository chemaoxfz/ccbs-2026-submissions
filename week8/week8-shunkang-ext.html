From parameter sensitivity to a minimal energy module

A whole-cell boundary test before protein design

CCBS · EST 5053 · Lectures 15–16 · Week 8 · 2026-11-02

The claim. Protein design should follow a quantified bottleneck. A minimal specification states converter capacity, routing efficiency and consumer capacity together with resource and damage constraints. Sensitivity prioritizes experiments; it is not causal proof or validated protein performance.

1 · Problem and scope

I retain input, conversion, routing, consumer capacity and one cost term. This is smaller than a whole-cell model but must close energy/resource bookkeeping. The output is a feasible design region and a falsifiable failure condition, not a protein sequence.

2 · Design model

J_source=min(J_input,k_cat,aux E_aux,J_acceptor,J_membrane)
J_useful=min(η_route J_source,V_consumer,J_substrate)
C=c_expr E_aux+c_damage D(J_source,E_aux).

Use J_input=4, k_cat,aux=10, J_acceptor=5, J_membrane=8, η_route=0.70 and V_consumer=3. A finite-difference sensitivity is S_p≈[J(p(1+δ))−J(p(1−δ))]/(2δp), δ=0.05. At E_aux≥0.4 the source is input-limited; increasing route efficiency can still help until consumer capacity, while increasing consumer capacity has zero local benefit at this point.

3 · Assay and controls

Scan converter abundance, route efficiency and consumer capacity with measured or literature ranges. Measure source input, local carrier, consumer flux, abundance, substrate/acceptor use, growth and damage in the same window.

Bootstrap biological replicates or sample posterior parameters to rank sensitivities. Hold one perturbation out for validation; the same data should not both identify and test the design.

4 · Result and minimal specification

For J_useful≥2.7 with J_input=4 and V_consumer≥3, one feasible specification is η_route≥0.675 and k_cat,aux E_aux≥4; at η=0.75, E_aux≥0.36 is sufficient in this upper-bound model. This is a flux requirement, not proof that a protein with those properties exists.

5 · Falsification and next test

Prediction. If V_consumer is capped at 2, increasing η_route or E_aux after J_useful=2 must not increase target flux. If it does, the capacity measurement or model is wrong. Conversely, if a high-sensitivity parameter raises source/local carrier but not target output, routing, leakage or a hidden sink is missing.

Local sensitivity is not causality, and min models hide smooth competition. Whole-cell closure needs measured stoichiometry, resource allocation and damage. Next, perturb the highest- and a deliberately low-ranked parameter in a held-out condition; selective response supports the ranking.
