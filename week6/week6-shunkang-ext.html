Does extra energy change a reaction-order regime, or only the gain?

Local carrier elasticity at one consumer

CCBS · EST 5053 · Lectures 11–12 · Week 6 · 2026-10-19

The claim. If an auxiliary route changes the carrier level seen by one consumer while substrate and consumer capacity are fixed, marginal benefit is largest in the carrier-limited range and approaches zero at saturation. The local elasticity nE, not absolute output, is the diagnostic.

1 · Problem and scope

Let E be a defined chemical-potential-bearing carrier: a local PMF term or ATP/ADP/Pi chemical potential, not ATP concentration alone. Source flux, routing, substrate and capacity remain separate bookkeeping terms. This is a one-consumer extension, not a whole-cell computation claim.

2 · Minimal model

Jc(E,S)=VmaxE/(KE+E)·S/(KS+S)
Jsource=min(Jinput,kcat,auxEaux,Jacceptor,Jmembrane)
Jconsumer=min[Jc,Jsubstrate,Jcapacity].
nE=∂logJc/∂logE=KE/(KE+E).

The null predicts nE=0.909 at E=0.1KE, 0.500 at E=KE, and 0.091 at E=10KE. Values above one would falsify this one-site null rather than being clipped.

3 · Assay, controls and statistics

Use at least five input levels below, near and above KE, plus no source. In the same window measure source input, calibrated Elocal, target flux, substrate, consumer abundance, growth and damage. Use at least three independent biological replicates on separate days; cells are nested observations.

Fit log J_consumer ~ log E_local × condition + log S + log Vmax + (1|day/replicate); report slope and 95% CI. A finite-difference check uses [logJ(Eq)−logJ(E/q)]/[2logq] with q pre-registered.

4 · Illustrative result

If output rises but nE is unchanged, report a gain or capacity change. If source-side carrier rises while local carrier and target flux do not, route loss, leakage or native competition is more likely.

5 · Falsification and limits

Prediction. A carrier-limited preparation must show positive low-E slope and a lower high-E slope. If nE remains zero after substrate and capacity are non-limiting, the model or carrier proxy is falsified. Reproducible nE>1 demands a different mechanism.

Elasticity is local. PMF, ATP chemical potential and redox potential cannot share an arbitrary concentration axis. A min model is an upper bound and non-smooth at switches. Reporter toxicity, photodamage and altered consumer abundance can mimic sensitivity. Elasticity alone does not show memory or computation.
