# Research essay: due Tuesday 2026-11-10, 23:59

This is one week after the final lecture on 3 November. The time zone is Asia/Shanghai.

Add one file: `research-essay/<yourname>.html`, lowercase, no spaces.

A standalone, self-contained HTML page on a topic you actually care about, readable on its
own by someone who was not in this class.

**Start with the `research-essay` (idea-to-essay) pipeline**
(<https://github.com/chemaoxfz/idea-to-essay>), not because its output is the essay, but
because running it is how you find out what your idea actually is. It sweeps the literature,
fetches the full texts and shows you where your topic sits among what people have already
said. Once you can see that, you can formulate the PCAPS chain that follows, and formulating
it is the real work.

Worth 20 points, and they are for doing these four things honestly rather than for being
brilliant at them:

| Criterion |
|---|
| The PCAPS logic is cleanly presented (problem, challenge, approach, proof, significance) |
| The literature and field context are clear, and the topic is visibly in conversation with them |
| There is real insight, obtained and analyzed with some depth |
| The presentation is very clear: tutorial-like, readable by any student in the class, well illustrated |

Talk to the lecturer about your topic before the middle of October. A badly chosen topic is
the most common reason a strong essay is not possible.
